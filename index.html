about me

I’m Vanikalyani, a multidisciplinary designer from India, drawn to Indian art and culture and to the calm that good design can bring. I move between product, UI and research, and I care about the quiet details that make an experience feel effortless.

I love making things and keeping myself occupied with anything new that excites my mind. Design, for me, is less about decoration and more about clarity: giving people a little more ease in their day.
